A story in the wood

From a Loire valley wood pile to eight family bread knives

A piece of French oak came home from an innkeeper named Jean Claude. From that wood: eight bread knives, one for each child, plus Grandma. Each knife carries a laser engraving of the story.

That is the heart of Papsdadee-o. Wood with a past, made into something a family can use and pass on.

Caring for Your Piece

A little care goes a long way

  • Hand wash with mild soap and warm water.
  • Dry soon after washing. Do not soak.
  • Skip the dishwasher.
  • Rub on a thin coat of food-safe oil when the wood looks dry.
