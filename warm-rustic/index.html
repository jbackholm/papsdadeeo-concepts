Make something from wood you love

Have a piece of Grandpa's apple tree, a dining table leg, or wood from a trip that still means something? It can become a bread knife for each person in the family, laser engraved with the short story of the wood.

One example: French oak brought home from a wood pile in the Loire valley, from an innkeeper named Jean Claude. Eight bread knives came from it, one for each child, plus Grandma. Each carries the story engraved in the wood.

Caring for Your Piece

Hand wash. Dry soon after. A light coat of food-safe oil now and then keeps the wood happy. Avoid the dishwasher and long soaks.
