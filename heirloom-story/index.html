A story told like a museum wall label: wood with a past, made into pieces a family can keep.

The Loire Oak Knives

Eight knives from one piece of French oak

A piece of French oak came home from a wood pile in the Loire valley, from an innkeeper named Jean Claude. From that wood: eight bread knives, one for each child, plus Grandma.

Each knife carries a laser engraving of the story. That is the custom work this site opens the door to.

Caring for Your Piece

  • Hand wash with mild soap and warm water.
  • Dry soon after washing. Do not soak.
  • Skip the dishwasher.
  • Rub on a thin coat of food-safe oil when the wood looks dry.
