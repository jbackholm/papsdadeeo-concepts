A little book of wood, work, and family stories

This homepage is meant to feel like a keepsake journal. No shop cart. Just the maker, the pieces, and a quiet invitation to turn treasured wood into something a family can hold.

A letter from the Loire

On a piece of French oak

I brought home a piece of French oak from a wood pile in the Loire valley, from an innkeeper named Jean Claude. From that wood I made eight bread knives, one for each of my children, plus Grandma.

Each knife has a laser engraving of this story about the wood. That is the heart of the custom work I hope to do: take wood a family holds near and dear, and turn it into pieces they can use and pass on.

With care,
the maker

Caring for Your Piece

  • Hand wash with mild soap and warm water.
  • Dry soon after washing. Do not soak.
  • Skip the dishwasher.
  • Rub on a thin coat of food-safe oil when the wood looks dry.
